## Welcome to the Forge

Shiori Forge is an independent, open-source-first engineering initiative at the intersection of **infrastructure, security, artificial intelligence and developer experience**.

We build the tools we wish existed: software that respects its users, runs on infrastructure they control, and makes complicated systems feel a little more human.

Inspired by Japanese aesthetics and the discipline of a blacksmith, our approach is simple: question the defaults, build with care, test what matters, and keep refining.

> Ideas begin in chaos. Good engineering gives them form.

## What we believe in

| Principle | What it means at the Forge |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Open by design** | Favor open standards, transparent implementations and meaningful user control. |
| **Security from the start** | Design with least privilege, explicit trust boundaries and responsible disclosure in mind. |
| **Self-hosted freedom** | Make local and independently operated infrastructure a first-class option wherever practical. |
| **Evidence over assumptions** | Test real behavior, document limitations, and distinguish verified results from aspirations. |
| **Craft with character** | Care about reliability, maintainability, usability and the identity of the software we create. |

## Build with us

Explore the public repositories, read their documentation, and open thoughtful issues or pull requests where contributions are welcome. Each project defines its own contribution process, license and security-reporting instructions.

Please use a project's designated private reporting channel for security vulnerabilities rather than disclosing sensitive details in public issues. Never submit credentials or private data.
